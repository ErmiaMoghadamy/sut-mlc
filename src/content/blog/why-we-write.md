---
title: Why we write in public
description: A longer note on why SUT MLC keeps a weblog next to talks and workshops.
pubDate: 2026-10-12
kind: article
---

Talks disappear after the room empties. A short article stays, and someone who missed the session can still catch the idea.

We keep two kinds of writing. Short posts are for dates, open seats, and reminders. Articles are for explanations: what we discussed, what we still do not understand, and what to read next.

If you want to contribute, start with a short post. If the topic still needs room, turn it into an article.
